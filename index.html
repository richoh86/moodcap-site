Live vibe camera

Point the camera and watch the energy meter fill and the mood colour shift, in real time, read entirely on your device. Switch on Auto and MoodCap takes the shot at your peak, so you stop guessing the timing.

The card

Then AI writes what that face is actually thinking — one line, laid out on a 9:16 card built for your story, with a mood, three hashtags and an energy score. Me today, your pet, or the two of you with a chemistry score.

03

Mood timeline

Every card is saved on your phone into a private diary. Each month becomes a palette — one colour square per day — so a whole month of moods fits on a single screen. Nothing is uploaded: the timeline never leaves your phone.

Real cards, straight out of the app

The lines are not generic. Each one is written from what is actually in your photo — the messy bun, the third coffee, the guilty dog face. Pick your caption language in Settings: 39 to choose from, your phone’s language by default.
