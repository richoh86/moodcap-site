MoodCap: Vibe Camera & Diary

Point the camera and the mood of the moment shows up live on the preview; press the shutter and MoodCap writes the one line you didn't say out loud, then keeps every card in a private diary that turns each month into a colour palette.

Download on the App Store

iPhone · iOS 17 or later · free every day · no account, no sign-up

One photo becomes today. Thirty of them become a picture of your month.

01

Live vibe camera

Point the camera and watch the energy meter fill and the mood colour shift, in real time, read entirely on your device. Switch on Auto and MoodCap takes the shot at your peak, so you stop guessing the timing.

02

The card

Then AI reads the vibe in the photo and writes the inner monologue — one line, laid out on a 9:16 card built for your story, with a mood, three hashtags and an energy score. Me today, your pet, or the two of you with a chemistry score.

03

Mood timeline

Every card is saved on your phone into a private diary. Each month becomes a palette — one colour square per day — so a whole month of moods fits on a single screen. Nothing is uploaded: the timeline never leaves your phone.

Real cards, straight out of the app

The lines are not generic. Each one is written from what is actually in your photo — the messy bun, the third coffee, the guilty dog face. Pick your caption language in Settings: 39 to choose from, your phone’s language by default.

New in 1.1

The same live camera and the same private diary — now with more of you in every card, more ways to keep it, and a reason to come back tomorrow.

Profiles and tones

Tell MoodCap who is in the photo: you, up to three pets, or the two of you. Give each one a name, three traits and a short note, and the line starts to sound like it knows them.

Then pick how it talks: Tease or Warm, free — or Sharp and Poetic with PLUS. Profiles stay on your iPhone; only the one that fits the photo travels with it, to write the line.

Card styles, sizes and a sticker

Three styles — Classic, Film and Minimal — each in 9:16, 4:5 or 1:1, so the same moment fits a story, a feed post or a square.

Or export just the line as a transparent sticker and lay it over your own story. Classic is free in every size; Film, Minimal and the sticker come with PLUS.

A widget and a daily nudge

Add a small or medium widget to your Home Screen and today’s card, or the week’s palette, is there every time you unlock. No card yet? One tap opens the camera.

A daily reminder is off until you turn it on. Pick the time and it nudges you once a day — and stays quiet on days you have already made a card. Both are free.

Monthly recap

On the first of the month, last month comes back as one poster: three representative photos, your top moods, the month’s palette and the average energy.

Open it any time from the timeline. The recap is built on your phone from your own diary; sharing the poster comes with PLUS.

Plus 10 welcome credits the moment you install. Every share you complete earns one more, up to three a day, and a reroll is free twice on every photo. Want more? A 30-line pack is one tap away, or MoodCap Plus for unlimited. No account, no sign-up.

Unlimited on-device with Apple Intelligence

On an iPhone with Apple Intelligence, MoodCap writes the line on the device itself: unlimited, free, and the photo never leaves your phone.

The timeline never leaves the phone

The live mood reading, your diary and every on-device caption stay on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded, and there is nothing to sign in to.
