Frequently asked

What does MoodCap actually do?

Three things. It reads the mood of the moment live on the camera preview, before you press the shutter. It then writes the one line behind the face on a 9:16 card, with a mood, three hashtags and an energy score. And it keeps every card in a diary on your phone, where each month becomes a palette — one colour square per day.

Is it free?

Yes, every day. Three cloud cards a day are free, plus 10 welcome credits the moment you install. Every share you complete earns one more, up to three a day, and a reroll is free twice on every photo. There is no account and no sign-up.

What is the on-device engine?

On an iPhone with Apple Intelligence, MoodCap writes the line on the device itself: unlimited, free, and the photo never leaves your phone.

How do I get more cards?

Credit packs of 30, 100 or 300 are one tap away. Tap your caption balance on the Home screen, or open Settings › Get more captions.

Can I change the caption language?

Yes — 39 to choose from, and your phone’s language is the default. Open Settings › Caption language and pick the one you want.

What happens to my photos?

Cloud captions are off until you allow them. When they are on, a photo is sent only to write its line and is never stored. The live mood reading, your timeline and every on-device caption never leave your phone — nothing about the diary is uploaded. The privacy policy has the detail.

Are the moods and scores real?

They are entertainment. MoodCap is for fun, not a psychological or medical assessment, and nothing it writes should be read as one.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app. Your diary, your preferences and your settings live in the app’s own storage on the phone and go with it. There is no account to close, because there is no account. Anything else, email richohios@gmail.com.
