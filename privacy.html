Privacy Policy

MoodCap: Vibe Camera & Diary · Last updated 26 October 2026

MoodCap processes live mood reading, your journal and video editing on your device. When you choose cloud caption generation, the selected photo or representative video frame and applicable caption settings are sent to our caption service and AI provider. Separate service, purchase and optional usage-statistics data are described below. We do not use these data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Two ways to write a caption

On-device. On iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, captions can be written entirely on the phone. In that mode the image and profile are not uploaded for caption generation. Separate service and usage-statistics traffic is described below.

Cloud. Cloud captions are off until you allow them in the app. When you do, each photo you choose is sent securely to our server, which passes it to Anthropic’s Claude API to write the caption and returns the result. Our caption server processes the image and applicable profile in memory for the request and does not persist those contents in its application database. Anthropic handles API inputs under its applicable privacy policy and commercial terms, including any applicable abuse-prevention retention. Our server runs on Cloudflare’s infrastructure, which relays the request under its privacy policy. Finished cards are saved only where you choose (your photo library or the share sheet).

Video captions (from version 1.3.0)

Videos recorded or selected in the video editor are processed on your device for preview and export. MoodCap does not upload the full video or its audio to generate a caption. If you enable cloud captions and tap the generation button, the still frame shown in the editor is sent through MoodCap’s caption service to Anthropic (Claude), together with caption settings and any applicable saved profile described below. Existing request metadata and the random device code support the service and caption balance. Manually entered captions and on-device generation do not require this image upload.

Camera and microphone access support recording; photo-library add access supports saving. You can keep or mute the original sound. Saving to Photos or choosing a sharing destination creates a copy outside the editor’s temporary workspace.

The app attempts to remove temporary editing and export copies after the editor closes and active operations finish. After an interruption, it attempts to clear abandoned workspace directories the next time a video workspace opens. Unadopted import files become eligible for cleanup after ten minutes. Immediate deletion after a crash or file-system failure is not guaranteed. Copies you deliberately save or share are not removed by this cleanup.

If you choose Keep in MoodCap, the app keeps one finished captioned video and a still image from it in app storage on your device. If you choose Keep draft, it keeps one unfinished source video, a still image and your editing choices. You can remove or replace these copies from within the app. Copies you saved to Photos or shared elsewhere are unaffected. If you later request a cloud caption for a draft, the selected frame is sent as described above; the full video and audio are not sent for caption generation.

Profiles

A profile is the name, up to three traits and the short note you enter for yourself, a pet or the two of you. Profiles are stored on your device. When you make a cloud caption, the one profile that fits the photo or selected video frame (and the tone you picked) is sent to our server together with the photo, only to write the line. Like the photo, it is held in memory for that one request and is not stored on the server. With on-device captions, the image and profile are not uploaded for generation.

The device code and your caption balance

To count free captions and credit purchases without an account, the app generates a random code and stores it in the device keychain. Our server keeps, against that code only, how many captions you have used today, how many credits you hold, the Apple transaction identifiers of packs you have bought (so a purchase is credited exactly once) and, if you subscribe to MoodCap Plus, the date your subscription expires. The code is not your name, email, Apple ID, advertising identifier or any hardware serial, and we have no way to connect it to you. No photo is ever stored with it.

Purchases and MoodCap Plus

The 30-caption pack and the MoodCap Plus subscription are sold through Apple’s in-app purchase system. Apple processes every purchase and subscription, including free trials, renewals and cancellations; we never see your payment details. The signed receipt Apple issues is sent to our server, either to add a pack’s credits to your balance or to confirm your Plus status. For Plus, our server stores only the subscription’s expiry date, against the anonymous device code described above. You manage or cancel the subscription in your App Store account settings.

What stays on your device

Preferences, your cloud-caption choice, your profiles and the last balance shown live in the app’s own private storage and are removed when you delete the app.

Widget. So the Home Screen widget can show today’s card, the app writes a small snapshot of it (a thumbnail, the line and the week’s colours) to the app’s shared container on your device. The widget reads it from there; nothing is uploaded.

Reminders. The daily reminder and the monthly recap note are local notifications scheduled on your device. No server is involved, and they stay off until you turn them on.

The device code in the keychain can survive reinstalling, which is what lets your purchased credits survive too.

Usage statistics

The app sends us anonymous usage statistics so we can see where people stop between opening the app and sharing a card, notice when purchases fail, and run small product experiments (for example, which caption model a new user is served first). What is sent is a list of event names — app opened, photo taken, caption written, card saved, share completed, paywall shown, purchase outcome — with a few small details such as the mode, the card style, an error code or a product price. Never a photo, a caption, journal text, a profile name or a note.

The events are tied to the same random device code described above, together with the app build number, your device’s time zone, your preferred language and, from version 1.2.1, whether the build came from the App Store or TestFlight. They go to our own server (the one that writes captions), are kept there for 90 days and are then deleted. We use no third-party analytics SDK, no advertising network and no crash-reporting service.

From version 1.2.1 you can switch this off at any time in Settings → Send usage statistics; the app then stops sending and deletes anything still queued on the device. Versions before 1.2.1 have no switch.

Separate from optional client usage events, the caption service keeps operational and accounting records needed to provide captions and track usage. These can include the random device code, request time, charge category, model, experiment cohort, prompt variant and caption count, but not image contents or profile text. The usage-statistics switch does not disable these server-side service records; the 90-day period above describes client event records, not a guaranteed deletion deadline for every service record.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or registration
  • No third-party analytics, no telemetry SDKs and no crash reporting — only the first-party usage statistics described above, which you can turn off
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No cross-app tracking; our caption service does not persist photos or video frames in its application database. Provider handling and user-directed sharing are described above.

Children

MoodCap has no accounts and no way to know who its users are; caption requests use the random device code described above; image handling and provider retention are described in this policy.

Changes

If a future version collects anything more, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
