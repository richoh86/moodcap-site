Privacy Policy

MoodCap: AI Mood Captions · Last updated 16 August 2026

MoodCap: AI Mood Captions does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

The app may save preferences and progress in its own private storage. That data never leaves your device, is not readable by other apps, and is removed when you delete the app.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or registration
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No tracking, and no data shared with third parties

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If a future version collects anything, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
