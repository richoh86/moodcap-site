Support

PROJECT LINKFALL is a one-finger space combat game for iPhone. You steer your ship, it fires automatically, and you break enemy formations to progress through the sector and face its boss.

How to play

  • Relative movement. Drag anywhere on the screen. The ship responds to how your finger moves, scaled by the sensitivity you choose, within its flight area and speed limits. It does not jump to the point you touch. Movement sensitivity and ship height can be adjusted in Settings.
  • Automatic fire. Your weapons fire on their own while you play.
  • Lift to pause. Lifting your finger pauses combat. Touch and hold to start the resume countdown, then continue steering when it ends. Leaving the app also pauses the run.
  • LINK BREAK. Some formations are linked by a support node marked with a double ring and a diamond. Destroying it disrupts that formation's next coordinated attack and scatters the survivors. Shots already fired stay on screen. Defeating escorts before the node increases the node's score bonus.
  • Loadouts and difficulty. Choose a support module before each sortie. Clearing the sector unlocks Challenge difficulty.

Follow the in-game calibration and tutorial before your first sortie.

Accessibility

Settings include options for sound, haptics, flashes, background motion and movement. Menus are designed for standard touch targets. Combat requires visual tracking and continuous touch control and is not fully playable with VoiceOver.

Troubleshooting

  • If a run behaves unexpectedly, pause and return to the menu when possible, then start a new sortie.
  • If the app was closed during a run, reopen it. The game saves a resumable run when it goes to the background, where possible.
  • Progress and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the app may remove them, so avoid uninstalling as a first troubleshooting step.

Privacy

PROJECT LINKFALL has no advertising and no in-app purchases. Optional gameplay analytics is off unless you turn it on. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
